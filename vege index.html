Vegetables

Carrot

Carrots are root vegetables that are often orange in color. They are crisp, sweet, and rich in beta-carotene, fiber, vitamin K1, and antioxidants which are great for eye health.

Corn

Corn is a cereal grain first domesticated by indigenous peoples in southern Mexico. It contains essential nutrients including B vitamins, zinc, magnesium, copper, iron, and manganese.

Pepper

Bell peppers are fruits that belong to the nightshade family. They are low in calories and exceptionally rich in vitamin C and other antioxidants, making them an excellent addition to a healthy diet.
